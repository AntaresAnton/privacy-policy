Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the custom TikTok API automation and management application ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled safely and responsibly.

2. Information We Collect

When interacting with the TikTok API via our application, we may process:

  • Authentication Tokens: OAuth 2.0 access and refresh tokens to securely interact with the TikTok API on behalf of authorized user accounts.
  • Account Details: Basic profile identifiers (such as user ID, username, display name) strictly required for application functionality.
  • Content & Media: Video files, descriptions, tags, and media assets provided by the user for publishing, uploading, or scheduling.

3. How We Use the Information

The information collected is used exclusively for:

  • Authenticating and managing authorized sessions with TikTok API endpoints.
  • Uploading, scheduling, and automating user-directed content to authorized TikTok accounts.
  • Monitoring video processing status and upload logs.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share any user data or credentials with third parties or external marketing platforms. Data is transmitted securely and directly to TikTok servers via official TikTok Developer APIs.

5. Data Storage and Security

All API credentials and access tokens are stored securely in encrypted or local protected environments. We implement standard security practices to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of authentication data.

6. User Rights and Data Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • You can revoke access to this application at any time directly through your TikTok account security/authorized apps settings.
  • To request immediate deletion of locally cached tokens or logs, please contact the maintainer directly.

7. Compliance with TikTok Terms

Our application adheres to the TikTok Terms of Service and TikTok Developer Terms.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or data handling, please open an issue in our GitHub repository.
